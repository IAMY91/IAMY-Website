The idea behind AIRFLOW

Four perspectives.
One living whole.

How can we design organizations where people grow, intelligence serves a purpose and renewal becomes part of everyday work?

Flow Science informs conditions for good work. Biomimicry inspires adaptive systems. AI supports knowledge and coordination. Organizational Development brings these perspectives into everyday practice.

01 / Understanding people

Flow Science

Flow is the experience of becoming deeply absorbed in a challenging activity. I draw on insights about clear goals, useful feedback and the balance between challenge and skill to design work more thoughtfully.

In everyday workShared priorities, protected focus time, meaningful responsibility and room for recovery.
02 / Learning from nature

Biomimicry

Living systems adapt, connect diverse parts and renew their resources. Nature is my model, mentor and inspiration for asking better questions about how organizations are designed.

In everyday workDistributed decisions, short feedback loops, connected teams and cycles of activity and regeneration.
03 / Extending intelligence

Artificial Intelligence

AI can make knowledge more accessible, take on routine work and bring perspectives together. I shape its use with the people who work with it, with clear accountability.

In everyday workKnowledge assistants, decision preparation and less coordination work to free up attention.
04 / Designing together

Organizational Development

Together, we translate these perspectives into culture, leadership, structures and ways of working. We clarify roles and decision rights, test new routines and check whether collaboration, learning capacity and work quality actually improve.

In everyday workClear roles, workable decisions, shared learning and change that becomes part of everyday work.

AIRFLOW is my evolving design framework. Nature's principles provide inspiration; we test their value in each organizational context. We can design conditions for flow, but we cannot prescribe the experience itself.

What does regenerative mean here?

Work should renew capabilities and energy, strengthen relationships and improve the organization’s contribution to its wider environment.

For example: If AI helps with meeting follow-up, the time saved is protected for focused work, learning and recovery. Together, you check whether it actually reduces workload while maintaining quality.

The principles behind the approach
01

Agency

People can shape their work and make decisions close to where the knowledge sits.

02

Connection

Shared purpose, trust and accessible knowledge connect autonomous teams.

03

Antifragility

Small, bounded experiments turn uncertainty into a source of learning. Clear boundaries keep potential harm limited.

04

Regeneration

Work should renew capabilities, relationships and energy, while considering its contribution to the wider world.
