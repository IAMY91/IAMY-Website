# AIRFLOW redesign · 12 November 2026

Ilya Alexander Yacine remains the personal identity. AIRFLOW is his evolving framework for applying Flow Science, Biomimicry and AI to Organizational Development. The German site describes practical applications in focus, team collaboration, decision rights, learning loops and useful AI adoption. Agency, connection, bounded experimentation (antifragility) and regeneration guide the work. Flow is not promised; nature-inspired principles are framed as design inspiration tested in context.
