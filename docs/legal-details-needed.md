# Details required from the owner

The legal notice and privacy policy are still pre-existing drafts. They cannot be completed from the public professional information available in this project.

Required input: a postal address the owner authorizes for publication, the correct legal operator/business identity, and whether VAT registration or other business-specific disclosures apply. Do not infer a home address or publish private details extracted from a CV. Confirm the privacy notice for the chosen email provider before enabling server-side contact delivery.

Until these details are supplied, do not describe the website's legal notices as complete or reviewed. Do not merely remove the draft notices and imply completeness.
